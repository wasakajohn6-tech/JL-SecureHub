Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Information you provide

When you contact JL SecureHub through this website, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in and the details of your enquiry.

2. How we use your information

We use the information you submit to respond to your enquiry, understand the service you need, prepare quotations and communicate about a requested service.

3. Form processing

The website enquiry form is processed using Formspree. Information submitted through the form is transmitted to that service so your enquiry can be delivered. Please review Formspree's own privacy information for details about its handling of submitted data.

4. Sharing and retention

We do not intend to sell your contact information. We may use service providers needed to receive and respond to enquiries. We retain enquiry details only for as long as reasonably needed to handle the enquiry, provide requested services, or meet applicable obligations.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps to handle enquiry information responsibly. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details or other highly sensitive information through the contact form.

6. Your choices and questions

If you have a question about an enquiry you submitted or wish to request an update to your contact information, email wasakajohn6@gmail.com.

7. Updates

This policy may be updated when our website or practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
